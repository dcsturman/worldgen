Traveller Trade Computer

A free speculative trade calculator for the Traveller RPG. Choose an origin and a destination world, by name from TravellerMap's Charted Space data or by typing a UWP, and the trade computer works out a trading session using the Mongoose Traveller 2nd Edition trade rules:

  • Trade goods available at the origin world from its trade codes and population, from the 36-entry D66 trade goods table, with quantities in tons.
  • Purchase and sale prices for each good, adjusted for both worlds' trade codes and for the Broker skill on each side of the deal, so you can see the profit or loss before you buy.
  • Passengers by class (High, Middle, Basic and Low passage), with the Steward skill, and freight lots by tonnage, priced by jump distance.
  • A ship manifest that totals cargo, passengers and freight for the voyage.

Each ship is a shared session: everyone at the table who opens the same ship sees its markets and manifest update together, and the ship is saved between game nights, which makes it easy to run a merchant campaign online.

Related tools: the Ship Simulator runs a merchant across many jumps automatically, and the System Generator builds the star system around a world. All Callisto Traveller tools.
