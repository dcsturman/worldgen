Free tools for the Traveller RPG

Callisto Traveller Tools is a set of free, browser-based tools for referees and players of the Traveller science-fiction role-playing game. They read world data straight from TravellerMap, so any world in Charted Space can be used by name, and they work for worlds of your own making from a UWP.

  • Trade Computer: speculative trade goods, buy and sell prices, passengers and freight between two worlds.
  • Solar System Generator: a complete star system around a main world, with stars, orbits, planets, moons and settlements.
  • World Map Generator: a printable icosahedral hex map of any world from its UWP.
  • Ship Simulator: a merchant ship trading across a subsector for profit.
  • Pirate Simulator: a corsair raiding shipping, fencing loot and building a reputation.

Traveller is a trademark of Mongoose Publishing. These are unofficial fan tools.
