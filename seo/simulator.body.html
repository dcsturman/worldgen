Traveller Merchant Ship Simulator

A free merchant trading simulator for the Traveller RPG. Choose a home port on TravellerMap, set up your ship and crew skills, and a route planner trades for you: buying speculative cargo, carrying passengers and freight, paying running costs and choosing each jump, so you can see how a merchant fares over months of play. A captain's log tells the story of the voyage.

It uses the same trade rules as the Trade Computer. Prefer the other side of the law? Try the Pirate Simulator. All Callisto Traveller tools.
