Traveller Solar System Generator

A free star system generator for the Traveller RPG. Give it a main world, by name from TravellerMap or as a UWP, and it builds the whole system around it: the star and any companion stars, orbits in real distances and travel times, gas giants and planetoid belts, the other planets and their moons, each world's temperature, and outposts, mines and colonies on the worlds people have settled.

It respects published data: the stars, gas giant and belt counts and world count from TravellerMap are kept, and the rest is generated to fit. Every system comes with a system map, and each world can open its own surface map in the World Map Generator. The same system always generates the same way, so a referee and players see the same thing.
