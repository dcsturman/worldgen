Traveller Pirate Simulator

A free pirate campaign simulator for the Traveller RPG. Choose a hideout, set your corsair's doctrine, and send it raiding: finding prey, taking prizes, fencing stolen cargo, and living with the reputation that follows, from merchants who surrender quickly to navies that come hunting. It suits Trojan Reach and Pirates of Drinax style campaigns.

For honest trading, see the Ship Simulator and the Trade Computer. All Callisto Traveller tools.
