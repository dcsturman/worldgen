Traveller World Map Generator

A free world map generator for the Traveller RPG. Enter a world's UWP and get a classic icosahedral hex map of its surface: continents and oceans sized by its hydrographics, climate from its atmosphere and orbit, biomes from ice caps and tundra to forests and deserts, mountains, rivers, cities sized by population, and the starport.

Maps are deterministic, so the same world always gets the same map, and they can be downloaded as SVG or as a PNG ready to upload to Roll20 or another virtual tabletop. A rotating globe view is available too.
